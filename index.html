AI book assistant

A conversational AI chatbot designed to enhance book discovery and exploration.

Built with Ruby on Rails and powered by the RubyLLM gem.

Chatbot features

The chatbot maintains conversation history, references content stored in the database to guide users, and suggests relevant books not yet in the collection, giving users the option to add them.

It also provides insights about books and supports image uploads within the chat, allowing users to discuss visual content directly with the AI.
